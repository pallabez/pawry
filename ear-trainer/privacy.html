Pawry Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026 · applies to the iPhone app, Pawry: Ear Trainer. The Android app has its own policy.

Pawry has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It sends us how the app is used, tied to a random id, and nothing that says who you are. You can turn this off in Settings.

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or advertising code. It never reads your contacts, photos, location, microphone or advertising identifier. You can practise with no connection.

What stays on your device

Your practice answers, statistics, streaks, level progress and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device may include the app's data in those backups; that is controlled by your iOS settings and Apple, not by Pawry.

What the app sends us

When there is a connection, the app sends usage events to our server: that the app was opened, which onboarding pages were seen and the choices made on them (such as your instrument and goal), that the Pawry Full offer was shown and which plan was chosen, and that a practice session or lesson started, ended or was left, with its exercise, level, number of answers and score. Each event also carries the app's version and build, the platform ("ios") and the kind of device ("iphone" or "ipad", never its model, name or any number belonging to it).

Every event carries a random id that the app makes on your device the first time it is needed. It is not derived from your device, your Apple ID or anything about you. It lets us see whether people come back and where they stop. We never receive your individual answers, your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. Our server does not store your IP address.

Turning it off

Settings → Your data → Share usage data. When it is off, the app records and sends nothing, and anything waiting to be sent is deleted from your device.

Why we collect it

To make the app better: which lessons are too hard or too easy, where people stop, and whether the free trial is clear. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Where it is kept

On our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare handles the connection, and so sees your IP address while delivering it, under its own privacy policy. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on your device, including the random id. After that, nothing links the events on our server to you or your device. Because the id is not tied to your name or email, we cannot look up your events by either; if you still want them removed, write to us through the support page and we will help.

Purchases

Pawry Full subscriptions, their free trial, and the earlier one-time unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Pawry never sees your payment details or Apple ID; it only receives Apple's confirmation of what you own, which is checked on your device. Apple's privacy policy governs the transaction.

Practice reminder

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. There are no push servers.

Children

Pawry collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the support page.
