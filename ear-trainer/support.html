Pawry Support

Pawry: Ear Trainer is a functional ear-training app for iPhone and Android. It teaches you to hear each note's place in the key, not just the distances between notes.

Get help

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports, questions and feature requests are all welcome. Please say whether you use the iPhone or the Android app.

Common questions

Where is my practice data?

On your device, and only there. Pawry has no accounts and sends nothing to us. See the privacy policy for iPhone or Android.

How do I restore my purchase?

iPhone: open any locked exercise or level and tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. It uses the Apple ID already signed into the App Store on your device.

Android: tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen or in the app's Settings. It uses the Google account signed into Google Play on your phone.

Does the unlock work for my family?

iPhone: yes. The one-time unlock has Family Sharing enabled, so everyone in your App Store family group gets it with your single purchase.

Android: the unlock belongs to the Google account that bought it.

No sound?

iPhone: Pawry plays audio even with the ring/silent switch on. If you hear nothing, check the volume buttons and whether your audio is routed to a Bluetooth device (AirPods in another room are the usual culprit).

Android: Pawry plays through the media volume. Turn it up with the volume buttons while the app is open, and check for a connected Bluetooth device.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. The iPhone app sends anonymous usage data, which you can turn off in Settings → Your data; the Android app collects nothing. Read the full privacy policy for iPhone or Android.
