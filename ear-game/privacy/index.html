Privacy policy

Pawry: Ear Game. Effective November 19, 2026

Pawry: Ear Game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It sends us how the game is played and the player name you choose, tied to a random id, and nothing that says who you are.

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no third-party analytics or advertising code, and asks for no permissions. It never reads your contacts, photos, location, microphone or advertising identifier. You can play with no connection.

What stays on your device

Your player name, trophies, matches played and won, your sound setting and your progress are stored in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

The game can also be played in a web browser, on this website. Everything in this policy applies to it in the same way. There it stores the same things in the browser's storage for this site, and sends the same two things with the platform "web". Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes what is stored there, including the random id.

What the app sends us

When there is a connection, the app sends two things to our server:

  • Your player row: the player name you chose, your trophies, matches played and won, the place you have reached on the tour, the platform (for example "ios" or "android") and the app's version.
  • Gameplay events: that the game was opened, a match started, a round ended, a match ended or was left, a training song was finished, or a name was set. Each carries game details only, such as the song, the kind of question, whether the answer was right and how fast, the score, and how many days the game has been opened on.

Both carry a random id that the app makes on your device the first time it is needed. The id is not derived from your device, your Apple or Google account or anything about you. It lets us keep one player's trophies together and see whether players come back.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. Our server does not store your IP address. The player name is whatever you type or the one the game suggests; please do not use your real name.

Why we collect it

To keep a copy of each player's trophies, and to tune the game: which songs and questions are too hard or too easy, and where players stop playing. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Where it is kept

On our server, hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare handles the connection, and so sees your IP address while delivering it, under its own privacy policy. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on your device, including the random id. After that, nothing links the row on our server to you or your device. To have the server's row removed as well, write to us at pawryapp@gmail.com with your player name and roughly how many trophies you had, and we will delete it and its events.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's player row in the same way.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us at pawryapp@gmail.com.
