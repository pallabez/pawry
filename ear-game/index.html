An ear training game for musicians.

Tap a kitten. Or wake the cat, if you dare.

How a round goes

  1. 1

    The amp plays

    A song everyone knows, so you already hear how it should go.

  2. 2

    The kittens play along

    A, B, C and D take a phrase each. One of them slips: one note that clashes with the amp.

  3. 3

    You tap the kitten

    One listen, and five seconds to answer. Three rounds against three players.

Real ear training

Hearing one wrong note against a reference is what a musician's ear does all day. The game makes it a match. New cats bring new questions: Jeni asks who played a wrong note, Aury asks who is out of tune.
