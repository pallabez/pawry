Help with Pawry: Ear Game

Four kittens play a song you know, one of them plays a wrong note, and you tap the one you heard. If something in the game is not working, this is the page.

Get help

Write to us at pawryapp@gmail.com. Bug reports, questions and ideas are all welcome. Please say which phone you use.

Email us

If you use GitHub, you can also open an issue there, and say that you play the Ear Game.

Common questions

No sound in the game?

Check the volume buttons, the Sound button on the game's home screen, and whether your audio is going to a Bluetooth device.

Can I get my trophies back on a new phone?

Not yet. Trophies live on the phone the game was played on, and a reinstall starts again.

Do I need a connection to play?

No. You can play with no connection.

What does the game collect?

How the game is played and the player name you choose, tied to a random id. No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
